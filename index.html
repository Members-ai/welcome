The founding five
  • Separation of duty, 1987. No critical step is taken by one member alone.
  • Fail-safe defaults, 1975. Access is denied unless it is explicitly granted.
  • Least privilege, 1975. Every user and program holds only the access its task requires.
  • Biba, 1977. A lower-integrity subject can never modify higher-integrity data.
  • Clark–Wilson, 1987. Data changes through well-formed transactions.
